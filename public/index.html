What to Watch Each Month

Around the 25th: Check if unlock tokens flow to exchange hot wallets (above) or stay in DeFi/staking. Feb was the first month they didn't dump — see if the pattern holds.

Big whale (0x790c42...): Sitting on 54.09M XPL bought at $0.05. At current price that's ~$6.2M. At $0.05 cost basis, any price is profit. If this wallet starts moving to exchanges, it's a major sell signal.

Jul 28 — US Public Sale: These buyers paid $0.05 and have been locked for 12 months. At $0.115, that's still a 2.3x. Many will sell. Monitor exchange inflows in late July.

Sep 25 — The Big One: ~1.67B XPL from team + investors hits the market. This is 75% of the current circulating supply added in a single day. Team tokens have zero cost basis. Even with a lockup, the overhang alone may suppress price leading up to it.

Ecosystem fund selling pattern: They already sold 600M+ of the 800M launch allocation. Monthly unlocks add 88.89M more each month. If Feb's "hold" pattern continues, the ecosystem selling may be winding down — but the monthly supply still adds up (533M more XPL by Sep just from ecosystem).
