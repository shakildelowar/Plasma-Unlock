Team Vault / Ecosystem Selling — The ManaMoon Investigation On-Chain Investigation

600M+ XPL sent from team vault to exchanges via TWAP around launch
Independent analyst ManaMoon traced the Plasma team vault and found it sent 600M+ XPL to centralized exchanges around the mainnet launch. Breakdown: ~250M to exchanges generally, ~120M to Bitfinex, ~260M to other CEXs. 99M XPL hit Binance alone the day before TGE. The address is not publicly labeled but can be found by searching the "Plasma" entity on Arkham Intelligence.
Source: ManaMoonNFT on X, Cryptonomist
Wintermute involvement — 25K-60K XPL batches flowing to CEXs
ManaMoon identified XPL flowing through Wintermute-linked addresses into CEXs in batches of 25K-60K tokens. Wintermute's market-making activity picked up on the second day after launch — the same day XPL topped out at $1.68. Plasma founder Paul Faecks denied engaging Wintermute: "We have not engaged Wintermute as a market maker and have never contracted with Wintermute for any of their services." However, on-chain data contradicts this claim. No single confirmed Wintermute address has been identified, but the pattern of TWAP-style batched sells is consistent.
Source: ManaMoonNFT, CoinTelegraph
Team's defense: "Ecosystem & Growth" tokens, not "team" tokens
The team claims team and investor allocations are locked for 3 years with a 1-year cliff and that no team tokens were sold. However, community investigators noted this wording is careful — it doesn't address the 800M Ecosystem & Growth tokens that were unlocked at launch. The 600M+ that went to exchanges came from the ecosystem allocation, not the locked team allocation. This is technically not "insider selling" of team tokens but is still the team-controlled ecosystem fund being sold into the market.
Source: CoinMarketCap, Bitcoin Ethereum News
Plasma Foundation ETH-side vault held $500M+ during ICO
Per Arkham Intelligence, the Plasma Foundation's Ethereum-side vault held $345.2M USDC + $146M USDT + $7.5M USDS + $1.2M DAI (~$500M total) during the ICO period. This gives context to the scale of the operation and the capital the team controls.
Source: Arkham Intelligence (search "Plasma" entity)

Feb 25 Unlock: First Time Market Absorbed the Supply Verified

Net outflow of $822K FROM exchanges — people were withdrawing, not depositing
The February 25 unlock (88.89M XPL, ~$8.2M) was the first monthly unlock that didn't cause a dump. Key signals:
  • $822K net outflow from exchanges — holders withdrew rather than deposited
  • 73% of Binance top traders were long
  • Price pumped 18% in 24 hours despite the unlock
  • Whoever received the Feb tokens held or staked — didn't dump
  • This was the first time this happened since launch
This is significant because every prior unlock was met with selling. The shift could indicate: (a) the ecosystem fund already sold most of what they intended, (b) the price is low enough that selling pressure is exhausted, or (c) the tokens were deployed to DeFi/staking instead of sold.
Source: CoinMarketCap
