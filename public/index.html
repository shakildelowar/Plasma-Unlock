Research Findings — January 2026 Unlock Derived from public data

88.89M XPL unlocked Jan 25 at 12:00 UTC from Ecosystem & Growth
The January ecosystem unlock released 88,888,889 XPL (~$14.7M at the time) into circulation. This was the 4th monthly ecosystem unlock since mainnet launch. The tokens go to the Ecosystem & Growth wallet controlled by the Plasma team, designated for liquidity incentives, DeFi partnerships, exchange integrations, and adoption programs.
Source: Tokenomist, Plasma Official Docs
Price dropped ~52% from Jan 25 ($0.166) to Feb 24 low ($0.08)
In the 30 days following the January unlock, XPL experienced severe selling pressure. The price fell from ~$0.166 on unlock day to test the $0.08 demand zone on February 24, with bearish momentum pushing XPL down 12% in a single day. Futures traders were "extremely pessimistic" according to CoinMarketCap analysis. This suggests the unlocked tokens — or broader market dynamics around them — contributed significant sell pressure.
Source: CoinMarketCap AI Insights
Feb 25 unlock was absorbed — price surged 18% after
The February 25 unlock (another 88.89M XPL, ~$8.2M) saw a dramatically different reaction. XPL surged 18% in 24 hours. Net outflow of ~$822,130 from exchanges indicated holders were NOT rushing to sell. The lower dollar value of the unlock (~$8.2M vs $14.7M in Jan) and the already-depressed price may have reduced sell pressure. This could indicate the worst of the post-unlock selling from earlier months has subsided.
Source: CoinMarketCap AI Insights, CoinGecko
Whale activity: $47.7M unrealized profits from $50M USDT deposit
Large depositors (whales) generated $47.7M in unrealized profits from a $50M USDT deposit into the Plasma ecosystem. This activity predates the January unlock and shows that significant capital was already positioned in the ecosystem. These large holders are a key variable — if they unwind positions, it would add to sell pressure beyond just the unlock tokens.
Source: ainvest.com
Only Ecosystem unlocks are active — massive cliff event in Sep 2026
Currently, only the Ecosystem & Growth allocation unlocks monthly (~88.89M XPL). Team and Investor tokens (50% of total supply = 5 billion XPL) are fully locked with a 1-year cliff hitting September 25, 2026. On that date, ~1/3 of each allocation unlocks at once (~833M team + ~833M investor = ~1.67 billion XPL). This is the single largest upcoming supply event. After the cliff, another ~138.8M XPL/month unlocks from team + investors + ecosystem combined. Additionally, US public sale participants unlock on July 28, 2026.
Source: Plasma Official Docs
DeFi ecosystem: $7B+ stablecoins, 100+ protocols, but yields attract mercenary capital
Plasma launched with $2B in stablecoins across 100+ DeFi partners (Aave, Ethena, Fluid, Euler) and surpassed $7B stablecoin supply by Sep 28, 2025. Aave's Plasma market saw ~$1.5B in daily deposits. However, Sentora flagged whale concentration risk on Aave v3 Plasma — a few large wallets hold outsized positions. 10% yields on USDT farms attract capital, but this is often mercenary — it leaves when yields drop, adding to sell pressure on XPL.
Source: Blockworks, The Defiant
Current state: $0.115 (Mar 5) — 92% below ATH, ~40K daily USDT txs
XPL trades at $0.115 with a $257M market cap and $159M 24h volume. It's 92% below its ATH of $1.52 (Sep 29, 2025). The network processes ~40,000 daily USDT transactions — watch this metric as an indicator of real network utility vs speculative farming. The recent 27% weekly gain suggests a potential bottom may be forming, but the March 25 unlock and larger events ahead remain risks.
Source: CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap

How to Track On-Chain (Wallet-Level Analysis)

To identify specific wallet addresses receiving unlock tokens and their selling behavior, use these tools directly. Plasma is EVM-compatible (Chain ID 9745), so standard Ethereum tooling works.

PlasmaScan
Block explorer for Plasma. View top token holders at the XPL contract page, trace distribution transactions from vesting contracts, and monitor individual wallet activity.
Arkham Intelligence
Has confirmed Plasma chain integration. Entity labels map wallets to Plasma Foundation, Ecosystem Fund, known investors. Search "Plasma" to find labeled addresses and track their transfers.
Nansen
Smart Money tracking across 30+ chains. Use Token God Mode for XPL to see smart money holders, balance shifts, and exchange flow signals. Watch for net exchange inflows as sell pressure indicator.
Tokenomist (formerly TokenUnlocks)
Tracks the exact unlock schedule, historical unlock events, and projected future releases. Confirms 88.89M XPL monthly ecosystem unlocks and shows upcoming events.
Messari
Fundamental research and data on XPL including tokenomics, governance, team info, and investor details. Use for cross-referencing allocation data and tracking project developments.
DefiLlama
Track Plasma's TVL across all DeFi protocols. Watch for TVL drops that could signal capital flight. Plasma is ranked by stablecoin market cap — monitor stablecoin supply health.
